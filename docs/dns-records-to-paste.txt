--- TXT, _dmarc, ALREADY PRESENT, CHANGE IT BEFORE SWITCHING ----------------
GoDaddy auto-created this. The old Wix zone had NO DMARC record at all, so this
is new behaviour rather than something being restored.

p=quarantine junks any mail failing SPF or DKIM alignment. Mail sent directly
from Microsoft 365 passes. Anything ELSE sending as @thistlearchitecture.co.uk
(CRM, newsletter tool, invoicing, Formspree) starts going to junk silently the
moment nameservers switch.

Set it to p=none for the migration:

Type:      TXT
Name:      _dmarc
Value:     v=DMARC1; p=none; adkim=r; aspf=r; rua=mailto:dmarc_rua@onsecureserver.net;

Raise it back to p=quarantine only after mail flow is confirmed on the new
nameservers and you have checked the aggregate reports for a week or two.
