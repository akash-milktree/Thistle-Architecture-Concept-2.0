## Notes

- The published planning route, date and Go/No-Go for every study was checked
  against its Feasibility Overview. No contradictions were found.
- Every sketch published was checked for client names and postcodes first. None
  carried any.
- Only Windmill Lane shows an indicative end value, because it is the only report
  that gives one. Three others quote gross rent or shop income, which is a
  different figure, and Hathaway states the end value needs a broker.
- The Hathaway Road study is the only No-Go and makes the case for buying a
  feasibility better than the six approvals do: Croydon protects family homes
  below 130 sqm from HMO conversion, the house is 93 to 115 sqm, so an HMO would
  very likely have been refused. The study found supported living instead.
