# Thistle Architecture — Product Context

register: brand

## What this is

The marketing site for Thistle Architecture, a feasibility-first retrofit
architecture practice within the Thistle Group (with HMO Designers and HMO
Checker). Since July 2026 the positioning is practice-level ("Nationwide
developer-led retrofit architecture"), with the paid feasibility package as
the primary conversion path. The feasibility package page is the one hard-sell
surface; every other page informs and routes to it ("Book Your Feasibility").

## Users

Property developers, HMO investors, commercial-to-residential converters, and
deal sourcers. Time-poor, commercially literate, sceptical of agency polish.
They care about decision speed, planning risk, and financial outcomes, not
design awards. Grade 7 UK English, no hype.

## Brand and tone

- Business-like, factual, calm. Sister brand to hmodesigners.co.uk; shares its
  photo-led warmth and plain category-statement headlines.
- Reference bar: Resi (clarity) + Searchland (data) + Urbanist (premium).
- Anti-references: generic SaaS landing pages, hero-metric templates,
  stock-photo corporate sites, anything "salesy" outside the package page.
- Proof over inspiration: real drawings, real documents, real people. Every
  fact traces to a client document or sits on the confirmations list.

## Strategic principles

- Two-tier CTA funnel is canon (see DESIGN.md).
- Real material beats polish: a genuine sketch PDF outranks a generated image,
  which outranks stock.
- Completed-project content is currently placeholder pending the client's
  photography; layouts must not look empty when galleries are missing.
- The site must feel like one cohesive system: alternating section
  backgrounds, shared card language, consistent CTA behaviour.
